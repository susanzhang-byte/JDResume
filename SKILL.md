# JDResume — Evidence-First Résumé Drafting

## Non-negotiable principles
- This is an **educational exercise tool** to expedite drafting. All outputs are **DRAFTS that require human review and edits**. Never imply application readiness or guarantee ATS performance or hiring.
- **Never fabricate or embellish** employers, dates, positions, degrees, responsibilities, skills, AI implementations, production deployments, clients, metrics, certifications, or outcomes. Preserve distinctions among designed, tested, piloted, adopted, and deployed.
- **Preserve source résumé data by default, including every applicant-provided number and quantified achievement.** Applicant-reported is not independently verified, but lack of independent access is **not** grounds for automatically deleting it from an educational draft. Flag verification questions separately; do not silently erase metrics, baselines, ranges, dates, revenue, adoption or impact figures. Never invent replacement metrics. If a figure is mathematically contradictory or clearly misleading, show the discrepancy and request applicant approval before changing or omitting it; keep the original source claim in the audit. If the applicant asks to remove a claim, comply.
- Do not auto-submit applications or publish personal résumés.
- A job posting is evidence of employer requirements, **not evidence of applicant experience**. Never stuff keywords for missing qualifications.

## Intake
Obtain the source résumé(s), complete target job description, target U.S. role, preferred output (Word/PDF), page constraint, and whether original formatting should be preserved. If original is PDF only, disclose that editable layout matching is a reconstruction; request the original Word source if available. Keep user answers already supplied; do not re-ask.

If files are accessible, inspect their entire actual content and any visible page formatting rather than infer from snippets. For evidence gathering, ask **one targeted question at a time** and stop after material uncertainties have been addressed.

## Workflow (execute in order)
1. **Verify target requirements:** Extract role, responsibilities, must-have vs preferred technology, governance, interpersonal skills, and operational deliverables. Do not confuse industry buzzwords with actual requirements. If web access exists, optionally verify job posting and date; note if current listing cannot be verified.
2. **Apply the owner's attached résumé writing standards:** Read `references/user-resume-best-practices.md` as the default V1 editorial standard: strategic interview-focused marketing document; one-page/recent-five-year *prioritization*; clean black-and-white standard ATS headings; readability for both ATS and humans; full degree names and month/year dates; titled education bullets; strong action verbs; STAR-based achievement bullets; credible qualitative impact; well-labeled, accurate skills and language proficiency. Preserve original source information and layout unless the applicant approves material edits. Use credible current career sources for supplemental verification, but never claim a universal ATS rule.
3. **Inventory applicant evidence:** Build a matrix for each job requirement: directly documented; applicant-reported; plausible but unverified; not demonstrated. Track provenance (source résumé, interview response, document). Assess relevance and seniority without inventing metrics.
4. **Interview for substance:** Request concrete action, tool, workflow, ownership, deployment stage, evaluation method, stakeholder/user, and verifiable result. Ask a single focused question per turn. Distinguish familiarity with tools from hands-on project work and enterprise deployment.
5. **Build a change proposal:** For each role/achievement label KEEP, REWRITE, CONDENSE, OMIT, or ADD, with rationale. **Do not silently delete original information: employers, roles, responsibilities, dates, tools, metrics, growth percentages, revenue, adoption figures or other substantive achievements.** Preserve these by default. Ask for explicit permission before materially removing or altering an original claim; if a format/page constraint forces condensing, first seek approval or retain the figure and explain the tradeoff. Keep a source-to-draft preservation audit.
6. **Draft content:** Apply the owner's `references/user-resume-best-practices.md` consistently. Treat the résumé as a marketing document for a specific interview; tailor for ATS relevance *and* human decision-makers. Default to concise **Situation–Task–Action–Result (STAR)** thinking, with action verb + method + scope + documented/applicant-reported result in bullets, without inventing any missing component. Prefer strong past-tense verbs for completed achievements; preserve accurate current-tense wording for ongoing responsibilities. When no metric exists, use supported leadership selection, adoption, progression or foundational contributions. Use standard Education/Experience/Skills headings, full university/degree names, graduation month/year, descriptive labels on education bullets, consistent formats, and meaningful labeled skills/languages. Match job terminology only where accurate; no unsubstantiated keywords. Preserve original outcomes and avoid silently removing older experience under a five-year or one-page heuristic.
7. **Handle AI skills precisely:** List tools actually used separately from languages. Use dedicated bullets such as “AI Tools & Technologies” and “Languages.” A personal functional MCP prototype is not an enterprise production deployment. Do not claim governance, Power Platform, Azure integrations, formal NIST use, or other competencies merely because the JD mentions them.
8. **Preserve visual style by default:** Follow the original applicant's font, sizes, page margins, section order, hierarchy, bullet glyphs, indents, contact alignment, rules, role italics, tab stops and whitespace. Dates on employer heading lines should end at the **right margin**. For new or user-approved restyles, use the attached standards' black-and-white, single-column main-body formatting (Times New Roman or Arial are recommended), simple bullets and standard section headings. Avoid sidebars, graphics, headshots, decorative tables and columns. Default to one page when plausible, but preserve significant content and ask before cutting anything; 'last five years' is a relevance priority, **not** a rule to delete earlier jobs. If attachment guidance conflicts with requested exact original format, keep the approved original and disclose the tradeoff.
9. **Produce files only with suitable tools:** When Word/PDF creation is available, generate an editable DOCX and PDF from the same final version; do not pretend to generate downloadable files without actual file creation. If rendering or inspection tooling is unavailable, disclose that verification is incomplete.
10. **Independently validate rendered DOCX and PDF:** Check page count, font names/sizes, consistent section order, heading alignment, right-aligned date tabs, bullets, consistent indentation, orphan lines, clipping/overlap, top/bottom whitespace, and ATS text extraction. Compare side by side against the supplied original. If a one-page constraint is set, verify both; adjust layout without reducing text to unreadable sizes. PDF and Word may render differently; describe which software was used.
11. **Return a draft with audit:** Provide output links, an explicit HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED label, a short change log, preservation comparison for original quantitative facts, and a separate applicant verification checklist. Specifically identify any removed or modified source number and whether the applicant approved it. Do not claim a guaranteed ATS pass.

## Required safeguards
- Do not assume a résumé's figures are independently verified. **Retain existing applicant-reported figures by default in draft output**, while documenting any verification needed outside the résumé. If contradictory arithmetic, ambiguous units/periods or causation pose a material risk, request clarification and approval before rewriting or deleting; do not silently omit source data.
- Keep skills taxonomy clean: AI/technical, language, and other skills in separate entries.
- Don't expand interviews unnecessarily once evidence is sufficient to create a truthful draft.
- Do not include sample applicants' identifying information in public example files.
- Never equate “resume is one page in PDF” with “Word is one page”; render and inspect both.
- Do not use images, icons, text boxes, tables, or multi-column structure solely for decoration when they could complicate ATS parsing. Preserve an accessible original design unless it creates an identified issue.
- If the user asks to retain all original roles but the page limit conflicts, explain options rather than silently discarding history.
